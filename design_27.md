# Design 27 — PNG Editorial

## Portfolio position
- Gen-Z: **6/10**
- Enterprise / premium: **9/10**
- UX clarity: **8/10**
- PNG identity: **10/10**
- Motion: **4/10**
- Complexity: **5/10**
- Similarity target: **0/10**

## Design thesis
Create a high-end editorial/documentary homepage where Papua New Guinea is not a background motif but the subject. Think premium magazine, annual report and modern national brand — combined with practical telecom service gateways.

## First viewport
Use one extraordinary full-height PNG photograph with **minimal or no dark overlay**, paired with an editorial text column rather than text floating over the image. Logo/slogan clearly visible. Headline should be restrained and confident, e.g. “A network shaped by Papua New Guinea.”

Navigation remains clean and conventional; originality comes from editorial composition, not experimental controls.

## Page narrative
1. Editorial opening / brand statement.
2. Feature story: People — how connectivity supports daily life.
3. Service index embedded as an editorial contents page.
4. Products — mobile/home plans presented as clean data tables.
5. Technology — infrastructure/business/remote capability.
6. PNG geography / remote connectivity feature.
7. Business & government/enterprise pathway.
8. News / media as true editorial articles.
9. Self Care / Support utility band.
10. Footer.

This directly expresses Products / People / Technology as three chapters.

## Visual system
### Palette
- Telikom Blue `#0875C9`
- Editorial Blue `#185E8D`
- Pale Grey-Blue `#E2EDF3`
- Paper `#F8FAFB`
- Ink `#172C38`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Rule `#CCD9E0`

### Typography
Gibson preferred for brand/functional UI. Use very large editorial heading scale and generous line spacing. If a secondary serif is considered for prototypes, use it only if brand governance allows; default safer direction is Gibson-only with weight/size contrast.

### Layout
Asymmetric editorial grids, full-bleed images, narrow caption columns, pull quotes, wide whitespace. Avoid floating SaaS cards and excessive rounded corners.

## Photography
Photography is critical. Use authentic PNG people, landscapes, businesses and infrastructure. Captions should provide context. Avoid generic stock or heavily treated blue monochrome that destroys local visual detail.

## Service discovery
Editorial does not mean vague. Include a strong contents/service index near the top: Mobile, Home, Business, Remote, Devices, Support. Service pages should be one click away.

## Plans
Present plan data cleanly with typography and rules. One highlighted plan can use a blue background; avoid card carousels.

## Motion
Subtle image reveals, caption fades, sticky editorial headings. No WebGL, no aggressive parallax. Reduced-motion simply removes reveals.

## Chatbot
Professional fixed bottom-right; visually quiet until needed. Greeting should not obscure photography/captions.

## Mobile
Editorial reading order becomes linear and strong: image → caption → heading → service action. Avoid maintaining complex asymmetry on small screens. Preserve generous whitespace without forcing excessive scroll.

## Accessibility
- Captions/alt text meaningful.
- Text outside images.
- Strong contrast.
- Semantic article/section elements where appropriate.
- One H1.
- Links descriptive.

## SEO
Excellent opportunity for rich, useful content and internal linking. Avoid marketing fluff. Use approved facts and service descriptions. News/feature stories should have proper article metadata when productionized.

## Performance
Image optimization is the primary concern. Use AVIF/WebP, responsive sizes, explicit dimensions, eager-load only first critical image. Avoid loading full-resolution editorial photography everywhere.

## CMS
Highly CMS-friendly: Feature Story, Service Index, Plan Group, Technology Feature, Article, Utility Band. Editors should manage captions and image focal points.

## Must avoid
- Turning it into a lifestyle magazine with weak service access.
- Dark overlays over all images.
- Generic corporate stock.
- Repeated card grids.
- Decorative PNG motifs pasted without context.

## Success
The site should feel like a confident national institution telling a contemporary PNG story, while remaining unmistakably a useful telecommunications homepage.
