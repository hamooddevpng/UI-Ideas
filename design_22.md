# Design 22 — People × Technology

## Portfolio position
- Gen-Z: **7/10**
- Enterprise / premium: **9/10**
- UX clarity: **9/10**
- PNG identity: **10/10**
- Motion: **6/10**
- Complexity: **6/10**
- Similarity target: **0/10**

## Purpose
This is intended to be one of the strongest balanced client-facing directions. It directly embodies the Telikom design pillars **People, Products, Technology** while visually contrasting human PNG stories with infrastructure and digital capability.

## Core composition
Use a persistent split-screen language:
- one side = PEOPLE / PNG photography / human outcomes;
- other side = TECHNOLOGY / services / network / product interfaces.

The split can change proportion as the user scrolls. At moments the people side dominates; at enterprise moments the technology side dominates.

## First viewport
No conventional hero banner. Build a 50/50 or 55/45 split:
- left: full-height authentic PNG portrait/community/business image with minimal caption;
- right: clean white/light-blue typographic statement, Telikom logo/slogan, and 3–4 service pathways.

A central dividing line can behave as a signal line and subtly animate.

## Page narrative
1. PEOPLE × TECHNOLOGY opening.
2. Personal connectivity: real person/story paired with Mobile services.
3. Home: household/education story paired with home internet products.
4. Business: PNG professionals paired with enterprise technology matrix.
5. Remote: landscape/community paired with VSAT/satellite explanation.
6. Products: plan/device/service selection.
7. Self Care: task interface paired with customer-support human story.
8. News/community.
9. Footer.

## Visual system
### Palette
- Telikom Blue `#0875C9`
- Deep `#075B96`
- Soft `#D8EAF4`
- Background `#F5F9FC`
- Ink `#102C3E`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Divider `#C8DAE4`

### Typography
Gibson preferred. Large editorial headlines on the technology side; captions and story text restrained.

### Photography
This concept requires excellent PNG imagery. Avoid generic stock. Faces should not be covered by text. Use natural, bright photography rather than darkened hero banners.

### Technology graphics
Use clean diagrams, plan rows, coverage/service indicators and simple line art. Avoid gratuitous sci-fi 3D.

## Interaction
As sections enter, the central divider may move from 50/50 to 35/65 depending on focus. Photos can change with crossfade; technology panels update on the opposite side. Keep scrolling standard.

## Service presentation
Avoid repeated card grids. Use:
- vertical service index;
- typographic comparison rows;
- line diagrams;
- split tables;
- full-height image/text pairings.

## Business treatment
Business section should be especially premium: photo of PNG professionals/operations paired with a structured enterprise solution matrix and clear CTA. No dark navy slab is required; confidence should come from whitespace and typography.

## Self Care
Show practical utilities in a clean stacked task panel: Top Up, Pay Bill, Buy Data, Coverage, Stores, Support. Pair it with a customer-care/local-service image or statement to reinforce the People pillar.

## Chatbot
Use a professional but warm chatbot. Launcher sits bottom-right. Greeting: “Hello — need help with a Telikom service?” Keep it visually on the Technology side where possible so it does not cover photography.

## Mobile
The split becomes a deliberate alternating sequence: photo, service content, photo, service content. Do not shrink two columns to unreadable widths. Keep photographs at 4:3/portrait ratios with content directly beneath.

## Accessibility
- Image captions and alt text meaningful.
- Text never baked into images.
- Divider animations decorative only.
- Semantic order on mobile should match reading logic even if desktop alternates sides.
- One H1.
- High contrast and 44px touch targets.

## SEO
Excellent suitability for service gateways because each human story can sit adjacent to keyword-relevant service copy. Keep marketing copy concise and avoid unapproved claims. Include crawlable Mobile, Home Internet and Business links.

## Performance
Moderate. Responsive imagery is the major payload. Use `srcset`, `sizes`, explicit dimensions and lazy loading. Motion can be CSS + small IntersectionObserver logic.

## CMS
Each split section maps to a Story + Service Group: image, caption, headline, summary, links, optional metrics. This is highly maintainable if the split layout templates remain fixed.

## Must avoid
- Generic hero image with blue overlay.
- Equal card grids beneath every story.
- Photos that could be from any country.
- Sci-fi network visuals that overwhelm people.
- Green accent colour.

## Success
Stakeholders should immediately see a credible modern Telikom identity that is local, human and technologically capable. This concept should demonstrate that “distinctive” does not require sacrificing professionalism.
