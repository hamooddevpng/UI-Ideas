# Design 34 — Executive Telikom

## Status
Final endpoint concept specification for `34.html`. This represents the conservative premium extreme of the portfolio.

## Portfolio position
- Gen-Z affinity: **0/10 by intent**
- Enterprise / premium: **10/10**
- UX clarity: **9/10**
- PNG identity: **5/10**
- Motion: **1/10**
- Code complexity: **4/10**
- Similarity target vs current website / previous concepts: **0/10 in composition**, despite conservative tone

## Purpose
Create the most restrained, boardroom-grade interpretation possible. This is the opposite pole from Design 11. It should allow Telikom management to evaluate what “maximum professionalism, minimum trendiness” looks like without falling back to the existing website’s dated structure.

The concept must be conservative in tone **but modern in composition**. Do not equate corporate with old-fashioned.

## First viewport
A quiet, immaculate composition:
- large official Telikom logo with slogan clearly readable;
- one concise corporate statement;
- one exceptional PNG/corporate/infrastructure image in a defined grid area;
- a clean audience index: Personal, Business, Enterprise, Company;
- direct Self Care / Top Up / Support utilities.

No hero carousel. No animated gradients. No glass panels. No decorative 3D.

## Page architecture
1. Executive brand statement.
2. Audience/service index.
3. Personal & Home services in concise rows.
4. Business & Enterprise capabilities.
5. PNG/national role feature.
6. Technology & Remote connectivity.
7. Current Products / Offers in a restrained table/list.
8. Corporate News / Leadership / Careers gateway.
9. Self Care & Support utility zone.
10. Footer.

## Visual system
### Palette
- Telikom Blue `#0875C9`
- Executive Blue `#163747`
- Pale Neutral Blue `#D8E2E7`
- Background `#F7F8F9`
- Ink `#17272F`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Rule `#CDD6DB`

Use the Telikom blue sparingly but decisively. No green design accents. No gradients needed.

### Typography
Approved Gibson strongly preferred. Use a restrained scale: large but not fashion-editorial. Body copy should feel concise and authoritative. Avoid trendy outlined type, monospace labels, kinetic text or oversized 150px words.

### Geometry
- mostly square/low-radius structure;
- fine rules;
- balanced whitespace;
- no floating cards;
- no pill-heavy navigation;
- subtle button treatments.

## Photography
Use professional, authentic PNG imagery: leadership/business, customers, infrastructure, landscapes where appropriate. Avoid generic handshake stock photography. Images should be bright, naturally coloured and not hidden beneath blue overlays.

## Service discovery
Despite the executive tone, consumer services remain easy to reach. A clear service index near the top should link to Mobile, Home Internet, Devices, Business, Remote Connectivity and Support.

## Business / corporate content
This concept should comfortably accommodate:
- enterprise solutions;
- corporate profile;
- executive management / board links;
- news and media;
- careers;
- institutional announcements.

Do not allow corporate content to bury customer services.

## Products / offers
Use restrained typographic rows or a comparison table. Promotional content should look like approved information, not flashy campaign cards.

## Motion
Almost none:
- subtle hover/focus;
- nav state change;
- optional image fade on load.
Respect reduced-motion automatically by virtue of the minimal system.

## Chatbot
Professional fixed bottom-right with a quiet greeting: “Hello. How can Telikom assist?” Actions: Personal Support, Business Enquiries, Self Care, Contact. Avoid playful iconography.

## Mobile
The layout becomes a clean vertical executive document with strong service access near top. Do not hide utilities inside multiple menus. Tables become labelled rows.

## Accessibility
High target:
- strong contrast;
- semantic HTML;
- clear focus states;
- 44px targets;
- one H1;
- descriptive links;
- logical heading hierarchy;
- no text-over-image problems.

## SEO
Excellent content-first structure. Support canonical/meta/Open Graph/Twitter/schema requirements, one keyword-focused H1, crawlable CTAs, internal links and image metadata. Production content must use approved Telikom naming consistently.

## Performance
Excellent. CSS-first, minimal JS, responsive images, no animation libraries. This should be a production-feasibility benchmark.

## CMS
Excellent. Service rows, news, corporate links, leadership gateways and offers are straightforward structured content. Layout should tolerate updated news/product content without breaking.

## Non-negotiable Telikom feedback incorporated
- lighter/brand-led blue treatment;
- images remain visible;
- logo and “Connecting you anywhere anytime” are clear;
- chatbot fixed bottom-right;
- content/ease of understanding prioritised;
- green excluded from interface;
- Products / People / Technology all represented;
- homepage structurally differentiated from existing/current mockups.

## Must avoid
- Looking like a bank website template.
- Looking like the old Telikom site with better spacing.
- Dark hero overlays.
- Tiny logo/slogan.
- Stock corporate clichés.
- Decorative animation added later to “make it modern.”

## Definition of success
This should be the exact opposite of Sticker Signal: calm, conservative, credible and executive. Yet when placed beside the current Telikom website, the structure, spacing, typography and hierarchy should still look unmistakably new.
