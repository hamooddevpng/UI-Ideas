# Design 36 - The PNG Connection

## Core idea
A bright interactive visual portal instead of a conventional rectangular hero.

A large circular PNG image sits at the centre of the experience with useful service controls floating around it.

## Hero states
Three selectable / slowly rotating states:
1. PNG: **PNG. Stay connected.**
2. Mobile: **Tap. Top up. Go.**
3. Business: **Work. Connected.**

## First-screen actions
Persistent action dock:
- Recharge
- Pay Bill
- Self Care
- Get SIM
- Coverage
- Support
- Find Store

Additional floating actions reinforce the current hero state.

## Body
- Choose by picture
- Simple task steps
- Business & Government
- Official Telikom information

## Visual direction
White and sky blue, circular image geometry, friendly large icons, low copy density, bright PNG photography.

## Distinction
35 is a campaign carousel. 36 is a service portal with orbiting visual actions.
