# Design 38 - Signal One

## Core idea
A premium light hero built around one Telikom-blue signal ribbon that connects the main service families visually.

## Hero
First line: **Proudly PNG-Owned**
Headline: **One Telikom. Easy to use.**

The signal ribbon connects visible service nodes:
- Mobile
- Internet
- Business
- Support

Bright PNG photography changes softly while the structure stays stable.

## Persistent first-screen actions
- Recharge
- Pay Bill
- Self Care
- Get SIM
- Coverage
- Support
- Find Store

## Body
- Four clear service paths
- Visual service section with photography
- Important official links

## Visual direction
The most premium and enterprise-ready option in the set, while staying bright, visual and simple enough for a broad consumer audience.

No dark hero, no low-contrast overlays and no abstract technical language.
