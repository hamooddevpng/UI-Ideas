# Design 31 — PNG Modernism

## Portfolio position
- Gen-Z: **7/10**
- Enterprise / premium: **8/10**
- UX clarity: **8/10**
- PNG identity: **10/10**
- Motion: **5/10**
- Complexity: **6/10**
- Similarity target: **0/10**

## Core idea
Develop a uniquely Papua New Guinean modernist graphic system using **abstracted blue geometry, rhythm and pattern** rather than generic telecom gradients. The goal is not to copy traditional motifs as decoration; it is to derive a contemporary grid language from repetition, weaving, line, movement and local visual rhythm while respecting cultural context.

This concept should feel like a modern national design identity, not a themed tourism website.

## Cultural-use rule
Do not directly reproduce culturally specific traditional patterns, clan motifs, bilum designs, carvings or symbols unless Telikom has approved source/context and permission. The prototype should use abstract geometric principles only: stepped lines, interlocking bands, directional rhythm, repeated signal marks and topographic forms.

## First viewport
Create a bright blue/white graphic composition with:
- Telikom logo + visible slogan;
- large typographic statement;
- one strong PNG photograph integrated into the geometric system;
- abstract patterned bands forming service navigation;
- Personal / Home / Business / Remote as four distinct rhythm blocks.

No standard hero rectangle or dark overlay.

## Page architecture
1. Modernist brand composition.
2. Products chapter — services integrated into geometric bands.
3. People chapter — full PNG photography with graphic captions.
4. Technology chapter — diagrammatic lines/patterns representing connectivity.
5. Mobile/Home offers as modular but non-card-based strips.
6. Business / enterprise index.
7. Remote connectivity/geography feature.
8. Self Care tasks.
9. News / community / footer.

## Visual system
### Palette
- Telikom Blue `#0875C9`
- Structural Blue `#286F9E`
- Light Blue `#AFCFE1`
- Pale Blue `#E9F3F8`
- Ink `#153143`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Rule `#C3D8E4`

No green as a design accent.

### Typography
Gibson preferred. Pair enormous clean headings with compact labels. The geometric system should complement, not compete with, the wordmark.

### Geometry
Develop 4–6 reusable pattern primitives:
- stepped line;
- signal arc;
- interlocking bars;
- repeated dash band;
- topographic contour;
- offset grid block.

Use them consistently across sections.

## Photography
Use authentic PNG imagery with intentional crops. Pattern bands may frame or mask images but should not obscure faces/content. Captions can identify context when accurate.

## Interaction
- pattern lines may shift/draw subtly on hover/scroll;
- service bands expand to reveal descriptions;
- image masks can open cleanly;
- reduced-motion shows static compositions.

No 3D required.

## Products / plans
Avoid rounded cards. Use modular strips inspired by the geometric pattern system. Data and price remain plainly readable. A highlighted offer can use a solid blue block.

## Business
More restrained variation of the same pattern language. Use a structured service index and large business image. Do not introduce a completely separate corporate aesthetic.

## Chatbot
Fixed bottom-right; launcher can incorporate a simple signal/pattern mark. Panel remains conventional and accessible.

## Mobile
Patterns simplify into vertical bands and separators. Never allow decorative geometry to cause horizontal scrolling or reduce text space. Preserve direct service actions near top.

## Accessibility
- Decorative patterns `aria-hidden`.
- Text remains real HTML.
- Avoid patterns behind small text.
- Strong focus indicators.
- One H1.
- Minimum target sizes.

## SEO
Text-first semantic service content, descriptive links and normal metadata. Pattern graphics must not contain essential headings as images/SVG paths only.

## Performance
SVG/CSS pattern primitives preferred. Avoid raster pattern assets. Keep DOM complexity controlled. Responsive images and lazy loading.

## CMS
CMS controls content/images; pattern variant is selected from a small predefined set per section. Editors should not create arbitrary motifs.

## Must avoid
- Appropriating or inaccurately reproducing culturally specific motifs.
- Generic “tribal” decoration.
- Green/red/yellow palette drift.
- Turning geometry into background noise.
- Standard hero/cards hidden beneath cultural decoration.

## Success
The design should be recognisably modern Telikom and recognisably grounded in PNG visual identity without depending on cliché imagery or borrowed cultural symbols.
