# Design 35 - Telikom Pulse Light UI

## Core idea
A bright campaign carousel designed around recognition, icons and practical customer tasks.

## Audience
Mixed-literacy, broad PNG audience. The page should still make sense if a visitor reads only headings and action labels.

## Hero
Three bright carousel stories:
1. PNG identity: **PNG. Connected.**
2. Mobile: **More data. Less hassle.**
3. Business & Government: **Keep PNG moving.**

The image is separated from the copy so text remains readable.

## First-screen actions
A fixed white action strip attached to the banner:
- Recharge
- Pay Bill
- Self Care
- Get SIM
- Coverage
- Support
- Find Store

Each action uses a large icon, short label and large touch target.

## Body
- Most-used tasks as large visual buttons
- Service chooser for Mobile, Internet and Business
- Help section with visual support actions
- Official information cards for News, About, Careers and Tenders

## Visual direction
White, sky blue and Telikom blue. Bright PNG photography. No dark hero, no long text blocks and no text over busy photography.
