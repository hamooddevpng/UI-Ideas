# Design 35 - Telikom Hub

## Direction
A fresh replacement for the previous Design 35.

## Core idea
A telecom super-app style homepage where practical actions come before a giant marketing banner.

The first screen asks:
**What do you want to do today?**

Primary actions:
- Recharge
- Pay Bill
- Buy Data
- My Telikom

Secondary actions:
- Get SIM
- Home Internet
- Business
- Coverage
- Find Store

A smaller campaign carousel sits alongside the action hub.

## Why this is different
- no full-screen hero
- no seven-icon bottom dock
- no floating bubbles
- no dark sections
- no generic corporate card-wall structure
- campaign content is secondary to task completion

## Audience
Built for a broad PNG audience, including mixed-literacy users. The interface should remain understandable through icons and short labels alone.

## Structure
1. Service hub
2. Compact campaign carousel
3. Popular products and services
4. Official Telikom information
5. Footer
